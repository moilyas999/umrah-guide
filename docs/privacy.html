Privacy policy

Umrah Guide is an independent iOS app from DeskLink.ai (Mohammed Ilyas). Bundle ID: ai.desklink.umrahguide.

Data Not Collected. The app does not collect personal data.

What is stored

Your packing and ihram checklist ticks, your place in the Perform flow, and which steps you marked done are saved on your iPhone with Apple’s UserDefaults. They never leave the device. There is no iCloud sync, no account, and no backup to DeskLink.ai.

What is not used

No analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDK. No location. No network calls. No access to contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

App Store nutrition label

Answer Data Not Collected for every data type. Tracking is not used.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be opened as an issue on the public repository: github.com/moilyas999/umrah-guide. Do not attach identity documents.
