Support

Umrah Guide is a free, offline educational companion for iPhone (iOS 17 or later). It is published by DeskLink.ai and is not a fatwa service.

Using the app

Perform Umrah walks through ihram, tawaf, sa'i, and halq or taqsir. Checklist remembers ticks on this device. Duas shows well-known wording with honest source notes. About repeats the privacy statement.

Get help

For a bug, accessibility gap, or unclear source note, open an issue at github.com/moilyas999/umrah-guide.

Do not send passport pages, visas, or booking receipts. Support does not need personal documents.

Religious questions

If you need a ruling for your situation, ask a qualified scholar. This website and the app will not issue one.
